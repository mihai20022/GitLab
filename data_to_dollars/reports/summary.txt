Summary of the roadmap.

Preprocessing data:
- scaling
- get dummies for categorical columns with more than 2 values
- mean or median for the null values


Models used:

Linear model - only captures straight-line relationships. Coefficient of determination is 0.704
Lasso Ridge - Ridge model have been tested. Adds penalties to reduce overfitting. The coefficient of determination is 0.706
Decision Tree Regressor - Captures non-linear relationships and interactions. Single trees can overfit. Coefficient of determination is 0.75
